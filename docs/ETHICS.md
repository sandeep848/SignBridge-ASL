# Ethics, governance and human evaluation

SignBridge is a research project for isolated-sign recognition and fingerspelling, **not** a sign-language translation substitute or a replacement for interpreters.

Before any user study:
1. Invite Deaf and hard-of-hearing ASL signers as compensated collaborators and advisors; give them real input into task definition and error impacts.
2. Secure institutional ethics review where required; use accessible consent forms and document withdrawal procedures.
3. Collect the minimum necessary video or landmarks. Do not upload recordings to GitHub or third-party services without explicit consent.
4. Report performance by signing experience, dominant hand, camera conditions, light/skin-tone strata **only with appropriate consent and sufficient sample sizes**. Do not infer sensitive attributes from images.
5. Publish failure categories including unseen vocabulary, weak lighting, occlusion, signer shift, facial-expression dependence and confidence failures.
6. Include a simple way to decline prediction rather than showing an overconfident incorrect sign.
7. Obtain rights-holder approval before publishing pretrained checkpoints or redistribution of sourced datasets.

Informed human evaluation, ethics approval and community endorsement have **not** yet taken place. These must never be represented as completed.
